One sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday moment strange and new — a single lost sock reframing the whole street as the middle of somebody else's story, judged pairwise by an AI judge that rewards surprise.

## Entry

> A single sock lay on the pavement, and the whole street suddenly felt like the middle of somebody else's story.

(111 characters.)

## How it works

`main.py` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. It is fully deterministic — a fixed string, no network, no randomness, no time dependence — and asserts the 1–200 character gate before writing.

## Files

- `main.py` — the entry program (entrypoint).
- `LICENSE` — MIT.

## Run locally

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
